ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Trust is built on traceable evidence.

TruthLens AI is a student project concept for evidence-led misinformation investigation.

Why TruthLens?

Newsrooms, NGOs, researchers, and public communication teams face a constant flow of claims and viral posts. Verification takes time: investigators need to locate original sources, check dates and context, compare evidence, and communicate uncertainty.

TruthLens organizes this workflow so that conclusions remain connected to the evidence behind them.

Our principles

  • TraceabilityEvery recorded source has a link and a note.
  • Source diversityCompare primary and independent sources.
  • UncertaintyInsufficient evidence is a valid outcome.
  • Human reviewPeople remain responsible for final verdicts.
